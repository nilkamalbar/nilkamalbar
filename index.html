The Sinking Sundarbans (Ep. 04) Published: Oct 2024
Timestamp 14:32
Inadvertent Statement in Voiceover

"...Between 2012 and 2022, Ghoramara island lost over 45% of its total agrarian embankment per year..."

Verified Correction & Clarification
