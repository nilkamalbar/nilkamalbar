Editorial Philosophy

A dedication to ethical slowness, contextual rigor, and deep presence.

I work at the intersections of cinema, journalism, and ethnographic research. Over the past decade, my cameras have tracked the shifting estuaries of the delta, contested forest rights in central tribal belts, and the uncataloged grief of climate migration.

Rather than transient headlines, my work prioritizes sustained presence. I believe documentary filmmaking is not merely recording reality, but creating a sanctuary where marginalized subjects co-author their own visual legacy.

Human-Centric Lens

Informed consent, dignity preservation, and dismantling parachute-journalism habits.

Empirical Rigor

Marrying visual empathy with verified archival investigation and spatial geospatial data.

Core Focus Disciplines

  • 01

The Disappearing Wells: Agrarian Debt & Salinization in Bengal’s Periphery

Over sixteen months, our team tested groundwater across sixty-four tube wells in the delta belt. What emerged was not merely an environmental fallout, but an illicit informal credit racket binding debt-ridden farmers to private potable-water tankers.
